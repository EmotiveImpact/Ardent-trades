+ What we do

Ardent Compute makes operating software for firms whose work happens in vans, plant rooms and buildings rather than at a desk. Each product is shaped around one kind of operation and the people who run it.

The admin that eats evenings and weekends is where we start: booking, chasing, certificates, quotes and invoices. Our software drafts that work so people only check it and press go.

Nothing important happens without a person saying yes. Money and regulated paperwork always wait for approval, and every step is logged so the record stands up later.

How we build

  1. [01]

    Software drafts, people decide

    Quotes, reports, chasers and invoices are written for you. Anything that moves money or signs off regulated work waits for a person to approve it.

  2. [02]

    Every step on the record

    Who did what, when and why is logged on the job itself, so a certificate, an audit or a dispute can be answered in seconds.

  3. [03]

    Works where the work is

    Basements, risers and plant rooms rarely have signal. Our apps keep working offline and catch up the moment they reconnect.
